# Crosswalk changelog

Crosswalks are **independently versioned** from the OpenWright package (FR-MAP-05).
A report always records the exact crosswalk `version` that produced it. Each
control cites its primary source and the date the text was reviewed (NFR-MNT-02).

> **Trust escalation path (NFR-MNT-03):** v0.1 crosswalks are maintainer-authored
> and rigorously cited. They are pending qualified legal/standards-body review,
> which is a planned funded milestone before any "reviewed-by" attestation is
> added to a crosswalk's metadata.

## eu-ai-act

### 1.0.0 — 2026-05-28
- Initial EU AI Act crosswalk covering Articles 12, 13, 14, 26, 27, 73
  (minimum required by FR-MAP-02), sourced from Regulation (EU) 2024/1689.
- Timeline reviewed 2026-05-28: high-risk Chapter III obligations apply
  2 August 2026 under the enacted text. The 6–7 May 2026 "Digital Omnibus"
  provisional agreement (would defer Annex III standalone high-risk to
  2 December 2027) is noted but NOT yet adopted; the enacted date stands.

## soc2

### 1.0.0 — 2026-05-28
- Initial SOC 2 crosswalk covering CC7.2 (system monitoring), sourced from the
  AICPA Trust Services Criteria (2017, rev. 2022).

## nist-ai-rmf

### 1.0.0 — 2026-05-29
- Initial NIST AI RMF 1.0 crosswalk (MEASURE 2.4/2.8, GOVERN 3.2, MANAGE 4.1),
  sourced from NIST AI 100-1 (January 2023). Voluntary framework; mappings are
  interpretive (AI RMF subcategories are policy/process-level).

## iso-42001

### 1.0.0 — 2026-05-29
- Initial ISO/IEC 42001:2023 crosswalk (Annex A.6.2.8 event logs, A.6.2.6
  operation/monitoring, A.9.2 responsible-use oversight). Annex A numbering per
  public references; confirm against the official standard.

## gdpr

### 1.0.0 — 2026-05-29
