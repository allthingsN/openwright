OpenWright — independent in-browser verifier

This verifier runs entirely in your browser via WebAssembly (Pyodide). Your report and key are processed locally and never sent anywhere — it never contacts the report producer or any OpenWright server. The one-time WebAssembly runtime loads from a public CDN by default; for a fully offline / air-gapped verifier, self-host Pyodide and append ?pyodide=<your-base-url>/ to this page's URL (see web/README.md). It checks evidence integrity — it does not assert legal compliance or certification.
